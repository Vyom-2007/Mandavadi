What to Expect at Mandavadi Navratri: Venue, Passes, Timing & Etiquette

1. Location & How to Reach Nidhivan Party Plot

Mandavadi Navratri is hosted at the prestigious Nidhivan Party Plot in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Easily accessible via major arterial roads, the venue features lush, manicured natural grass lawns that ensure shock-absorption and slip resistance for Garba dancers.

2. Season Passes vs. Daily Passes

To maintain crowd comfort and safety, entries are strictly monitored via RFID wristbands and digital QR passes:

  • 10-Night Season Passes: Unrestricted access for all ten nights of Navratri with priority fast-track gate entry.
  • Daily Passes: Limited date-specific passes available for individual evening celebrations.
  • Family & Group Packages: Dedicated packages for families seeking reserved seating areas near the main lawn.

3. Ample Parking, Security & Crowd Safety

We prioritize a secure, family-friendly environment with:

  • Dedicated on-site four-wheeler and two-wheeler parking with traffic marshals.
  • Professional female and male security personnel and 24/7 CCTV monitoring.
  • Fully equipped first-aid medical booth and paramedic support on site.
  • Hygienic restrooms and purified drinking water stations throughout the venue.

4. Dress Code & Festival Conduct

To preserve the sanctity and visual harmony of Gujarati culture:

  • Mandatory Ethnic Attire: Chaniya Choli / traditional sarees for women; Kediyu, Dhoti, or Kurta-Pyjama for men.
  • Zero Tolerance: Intoxicants and disruptive conduct are strictly prohibited. Mandavadi is a sacred celebration of Maa Amba.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I purchase Mandavadi Navratri passes?

Season passes can be pre-booked online through our official ticketing partners (BookMyShow, Paytm Insider/District, AllEvents) or directly via the Mandavadi website portal.
