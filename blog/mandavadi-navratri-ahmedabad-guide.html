Mandavadi Navratri Ahmedabad: The Complete Festival & Garba Guide

By Mandavadi Editorial Team
Published: Aug 20, 2026 Updated: Sep 25, 2026 7 min read

Every autumn, Ahmedabad comes alive in a kaleidoscope of devotional music, twirling Chaniya Cholis, rhythmic claps, and joyous celebration. At the center of this cultural resonance is Mandavadi—a premium Navratri and Garba experience designed to celebrate the sacred traditions of Gujarat under the theme "Suryast thi Suryoday Sudhi Maa ni Aradhana" (From Sunset to Sunrise, in Worship of the Divine Mother).

Whether you are an experienced Garba player who knows every step of Dodhiyu and Hinch or a devotee attending your first Navratri celebration in Ahmedabad, this guide covers everything you need to experience Mandavadi to the fullest.

1. What is Mandavadi Navratri?

Mandavadi was established with a clear cultural mission: to revive the authentic, devotional, and community-centric soul of Gujarat's nine-night festival. While many commercialized events have turned to pre-recorded synthesized tracks and overcrowding, Mandavadi preserves the sanctity of circular Raas-Garba, pure acoustic mandalis, and family-first hospitality.

2. The Sound of Mandavadi: Live Acoustic Folk Music

Central to the Mandavadi experience is our lead vocalist, Nishad Soni, accompanied by a master folk orchestra of double-headed acoustic dholis, harmonium, and traditional percussionists.

Every evening begins with sacred Stutis and the divine Maha Aarti, transitioning seamlessly into graceful 2-Taali and 3-Taali Garba, and accelerating towards midnight with electrifying Dodhiyu, high-paced Hinch, and traditional Gujarati Sanedo.

Authentic Acoustic Commitment:

Mandavadi features 100% live singing and authentic instruments. The musicians interact directly with the Garba floor, dynamically matching the energy and step changes of thousands of dancers.

3. The Venue: Nidhivan Party Plot, Ahmedabad

Mandavadi is hosted at the prestigious Nidhivan Party Plot in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. The venue features expansive natural green lawns, hand-crafted Lippan clay art installations, warm ambient lighting designed to be glare-free for dancers, hygienic Farali food zones, and dedicated parking.

4. Sunset-to-Sunrise Daily Schedule & Aarti

  • 08:30 PM – 09:30 PM: Auspicious Shankhnaad, Vishwambhari Stuti & Grand Maha Aarti honoring Maa Amba.
  • 09:30 PM – 11:30 PM: Classic 2-Taali and 3-Taali circular Garba.
  • 11:30 PM – 02:00 AM: High-Energy Dodhiyu, Hinch, and synchronized community Raas.
  • 02:00 AM – Dawn: Celebratory Sanedo verses, late-night folk hymns, and peaceful conclusion.

5. Cultural Attire & Dress Code Guidelines

To honor the festival's sacred heritage, traditional attire is strongly encouraged:

  • Women: Traditional Chaniya Choli with mirror work, Gamthi embroidery, Bandhani dupattas, and oxidized silver jewelry.
  • Men: Authentic Kediyu-Dhoti ensembles, traditional Kurtas, and colorful turbans (Pagdi).

6. How to Pre-Book Season Passes

Due to strict capacity limits ensuring ample dancing room and guest safety, passes must be pre-booked online through our official ticketing partners (BookMyShow, Paytm Insider/District, AllEvents) or directly via our website booking portal.
