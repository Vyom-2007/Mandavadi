Meet the Sound of Mandavadi: Nishad Soni & The Live Folk Mandali

An inside look at how lead vocalist Nishad Soni and master acoustic dhol percussionists create an organic spiritual connection with thousands of dancers every night at Mandavadi.

1. The Devotional Voice of Nishad Soni

At Mandavadi Navratri, the musical heartbeat of all ten nights rests with our exclusive lead vocalist, Nishad Soni. Renowned for his vocal range, emotional depth, and deep reverence for Gujarati Sugam Sangeet and folk traditions, Nishad Soni bridges centuries-old devotional compositions with energetic festival raas.

From the gentle nuances of opening stutis like Vishwambhari to the electrifying crescendo of Tara Vina Shyam and high-speed Sanedo, his performance sets the mood for the entire festival. Explore his full artist profile on our dedicated Artists Page.

2. The Master Dhol & Percussion Mandali

Accompanying Nishad Soni is an acoustic ensemble of master musicians:

  • Master Acoustic Dholis: Seasoned percussionists using traditional sheesham wood dhols.
  • Harmonium Virtuosos: Providing continuous melodic drone and ragas.
  • Sharnay & Flute: Adding ethereal folk melodies that weave through the evening breeze.
  • Manjira & Khanjari: High-frequency metallic chimes synchronizing every hand clap.

3. Reading the Rhythm of the Garba Floor

A live folk singer does something no digital recording can achieve: reading the dancers. When the concentric circles at Nidhivan Party Plot lock into perfect unison during 3-Taali or Dodhiyu steps, Nishad Soni prolongs the melodic phrasing, dynamically increasing the energy in symbiotic dialogue with the dancers.

4. Experience Nishad Soni at Nidhivan Party Plot

Mandavadi hosts all 10 nights of live acoustic music at Nidhivan Party Plot in Ahmedabad. With pristine line-array sound engineering calibrated for clear vocals and warm acoustic percussion, attendees enjoy concert-grade audio without ear fatigue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Mandavadi use pre-recorded electronic music?

No. Mandavadi is strictly dedicated to 100% authentic live acoustic music, featuring lead vocalist Nishad Soni, traditional dholis, harmonium, and folk percussionists.
