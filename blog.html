Navratri Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers to common questions about Mandavadi, Navratri timings, Garba etiquette, and passes in Ahmedabad.

Mandavadi is a premier cultural Navratri festival held at Nidhivan Party Plot in Ahmedabad, celebrated for its 100% authentic live acoustic Garba music led by Nishad Soni, devotional Maa Amba Aradhana, and vibrant community spirit.
You can experience live Garba singing by Nishad Soni and an acoustic folk mandali at Mandavadi Navratri, hosted at Nidhivan Party Plot in Ahmedabad throughout the 10 nights of Navratri.
Traditional Gujarati festival attire is required for the dance floor: Chaniya Choli with Kutchi embroidery for women, and Kediyu/Kurta-Dhoti for men.
Season passes can be pre-booked online through official ticketing partners (BookMyShow, Paytm Insider/District, AllEvents) or directly via the Mandavadi website booking portal.
